About this receptor

Preclinical binding profiles indicate affinity for histamine H1 receptors. In vitro affinity does not translate directly into clinical efficacy, potency, or a causal link to a particular adverse reaction.

Clinical context

The SmPC reports receptor affinity. It does not attribute somnolence or increased appetite to this receptor alone.
