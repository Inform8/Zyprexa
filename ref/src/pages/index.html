ZYPREXA®olanzapine · coated tablets

Evidence-informed treatment in schizophrenia and manic episodes in bipolar disorder

ZYPREXA® coated tablets · EU SmPC · Prepared October 2026 · Job code to be assigned for the target market

Zyprexa (olanzapine) is an antipsychotic medicine indicated for the treatment of schizophrenia and for the treatment of moderate to severe manic episodes. In patients whose manic episode has responded to olanzapine, it is also indicated for preventing recurrence in bipolar disorder, in accordance with the applicable product information.{{c:1:4.1}}

Approved indications in adults · EU SmPC 4.1

  1. SchizophreniaTreatment of schizophrenia. Effective in maintaining clinical improvement during continuation therapy in patients who have shown an initial treatment response.
  2. Moderate to severe manic episodeAs monotherapy or in combination therapy.
  3. Prevention of recurrence in bipolar disorderIn patients whose manic episode has responded to olanzapine treatment.

Clinical profile

A closer look at the clinical profile

Schizophrenia

Schizophrenia can affect perception, thought, motivation and everyday functioning. Treatment aims to reduce symptoms, support clinical stability and help patients maintain functioning over time.

Bipolar disorder

Bipolar disorder involves episodes of mood disturbance that can affect behaviour, relationships and daily life. Treatment objectives vary according to the current episode and the individual's clinical needs.

Pharmacology

About olanzapine

Olanzapine is an antipsychotic, antimanic and mood stabilising agent with activity across multiple neurotransmitter receptor systems, including dopamine and serotonin receptors. Its pharmacological profile is relevant to its therapeutic effects and adverse-reaction profile.{{c:1:5.1}}

Understanding this profile alongside the clinical evidence and approved product information supports informed prescribing decisions.
