Zyprexa (olanzapine) is an antipsychotic medicine indicated for the treatment of schizophrenia and for the treatment of moderate to severe manic episodes. In patients whose manic episode has responded to olanzapine, it is also indicated for preventing recurrence in bipolar disorder, in accordance with the applicable product information.1

Clinical profile

A closer look at the clinical profile

Schizophrenia

Schizophrenia can affect perception, thought, motivation and everyday functioning. Treatment aims to reduce symptoms, support clinical stability and help patients maintain functioning over time.

Explore schizophrenia

Bipolar disorder

Bipolar disorder involves episodes of mood disturbance that can affect behaviour, relationships and daily life. Treatment objectives vary according to the current episode and the individual's clinical needs.

Pharmacology

About olanzapine

Olanzapine is an antipsychotic, antimanic and mood stabilising agent with activity across multiple neurotransmitter receptor systems, including dopamine and serotonin receptors. Its pharmacological profile is relevant to its therapeutic effects and adverse-reaction profile.1

Understanding this profile alongside the clinical evidence and approved product information supports informed prescribing decisions.
